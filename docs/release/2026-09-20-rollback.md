# Rollback plan — FINAL PRE-LAUNCH RELEASE, 2026-09-20

## The one thing that is not obvious

**From the moment 0101 commits, Vercel's instant-rollback button is not a recovery
option — it is the failure.** 0101 drops `usage_events_member_insert` and revokes
`INSERT` from `anon, authenticated`. The OLD build writes the usage ledger through
the cookie-session (anon-key) client at `lib/services/usage.ts:79`, so with 0101
applied the old build cannot record a single billable event. Rolling the app back
alone converts a partial problem into a total billing outage.

So after step 6, **app rollback and 0101 reversal are one action, not two.**

## Reversing 0101 (exact, verified against 0012 and 0101)

```sql
-- restores what 0101_idempotency_key_is_released.sql:160-161 removed.
create policy "usage_events_member_insert" on public.usage_events
  for insert with check (public.is_workspace_member(workspace_id) and user_id = auth.uid());
grant insert on table public.usage_events to anon, authenticated;
```

Both halves are required. Recreating the policy without re-granting INSERT leaves
the old build failing closed; re-granting without the policy leaves RLS refusing.

> Reversing 0101 deliberately re-opens **P0-01** — the client-writable path into
> the credit ledger. That is the trade: a known, documented hole for minutes,
> versus every billable path dead. Do not leave it reversed longer than the
> incident.

## After 0106

Rolling the app back after 0106 leaves waitlist confirmation mail off in either
direction — the old build gates on `result.mail`, which 0106 stops returning.
Reversing 0106 means re-running `0097_newsletter_contact_merge.sql`'s
`waitlist_subscribe` body and dropping `waitlist_confirmation_payload`.

## 0109

0109 runs in one transaction and rolls itself back entirely on any failed
assertion — usually nothing to undo. To reverse a SUCCESSFUL 0109, re-issue the
bare `ALTER POLICY` forms (the un-hoisted expressions). **Never** DROP+CREATE:
that would change the command or roles and leave a window with no policy.

## Order of preference during an incident

1. Fix forward with a new deploy — always first choice.
2. If the new build is fundamentally broken: reverse 0101 **and** roll the app
   back, in that order, in the same maintenance action.
3. Never roll the app back alone after step 6.
