# Product Vision

GrovBase gives e-commerce sellers a complete, marketplace-ready product
photo session from a handful of phone reference shots — without a studio.

Principles:
1. Product fidelity beats artistic creativity (Product Lock: exact shape,
   proportions, colors, item count, buttons, ports, labels, materials).
2. Honest states — no fake generation; unfinished features say "wkrótce".
3. The prompt engine is GrovBase IP: prompts are stored encrypted and never
   reach the client.
4. Vendor-neutral AI: adapters + router; models are catalog data.
Target market: PL first (default locale), then DE/EN.
